Menu is a component that displays a menu that opens from a trigger such as a button. Passing an array of items to `items` allows you to assemble regular operation items, check items, radio items, submenus, and link items. Keyboard operations and focus management are handled by Menu in the Base UI.

## Basic usage [#basic-usage]

Specify the element that opens the menu in `trigger` and the item to display in `items` . In addition to strings, you can also pass React nodes containing icons to the item's `label` .

<Component path="menu/basic" />

Each item can be specified as `disabled` or `color` . `color="red"` is suitable for operations that require attention, such as deletion. If you need a separator line, divide the items into groups and it will be inserted automatically.

## Item types [#item-types]

Use `type` of the item object to switch the type of item to display. If `type` is omitted, it becomes a normal operation item. The selection status of check items and radio items is managed using `checked` and `value` .

<Component path="menu/item-types" />

| `type`              | Main fields                                | Description                                                               |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `"item"` (omitted)  | `label` , `disabled` , `onClick` , `color` | Normal operation items.                                                   |
| `"link"`            | `label` , `href` , `target`                | Items that act as links.                                                  |
| `"checkbox"`        | `label` , `checked` , `onCheckedChange`    | Check items with selection status.                                        |
| `"radio-group"`     | `value` , `onValueChange` , `items`        | A group that collects radio items.                                        |
| `"radio"`           | `label` , `value`                          | Items within the radio group.                                             |
| `"submenu"`         | `label` , `items`                          | Items that open child menus.                                              |
| `"group"` (omitted) | `label` , `items`                          | Groups with headings. A separator line will be placed between the groups. |

A group's `items` can contain regular items, link items, check items, radio groups, and submenus. The same item object can also be specified in a submenu.

## Appearance and disabling [#appearance]

`size` specifies the overall menu and item dimensions, `radius` specifies rounded corners, and `variant` specifies the main appearance of the item. `outlined` toggles the popup border.

<Component path="menu/appearance" />

Specifying `disabled` makes the entire menu inoperable. If you want to disable only a specific item, specify `disabled` for that item object. Changing `modal` to `false` allows you to keep the menu open while interacting with other elements on the page.

## Styling [#styling-hooks]

Triggers are given `sui_menu_trigger` , popups are given `sui_menu_popup` , and regular items are given `sui_menu_item` . Check items are `sui_menu_checkbox_item` , radio items are `sui_menu_radio_item` , link items are `sui_menu_link_item` , and items that open submenus are `sui_menu_submenu_trigger` .

Items have class names such as `sui_menu_item-checked` , `sui_menu_item-disabled` , `sui_menu_item-highlighted` , `sui_menu_item-outlined` , etc. depending on their state. Popups include `sui_menu_popup-size_md` , `sui_menu_popup-radius_md` , `sui_menu_popup-variant_naked` , `sui_menu_popup-open` , etc.

Groups are given `sui_menu_group` , group headings are given `sui_menu_group_label` , separators are given `sui_menu_separator` , and indicators are given `sui_menu_item_indicator` . Spacing and background color can be adjusted using the `--sui_menu_popup-*` and `--sui_menu_item-*` CSS variables.
