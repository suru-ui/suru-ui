HoverCard is a component that displays additional information when you hover over a link or button. You can present the content you want to check without changing the screen, such as an overview of the terms of use or the user's profile. Triggers and popups are automatically associated and open after a delay.

## Basic usage [#basic-usage]

Specify the element to be hovered over in `trigger` and the content to be displayed in `children` . Pass elements such as links and buttons to the trigger.

<Component path="hover-card/basic" />

Passing a function to `children` allows it to receive and assemble the active trigger's `payload` . If you want multiple triggers to share a single hovercard, pass the handle created in `HoverCard.createHandle` to `handle` .

## arrows and placement [#placement]

`arrow` displays an arrow in the popup. The placement can be adjusted with `side` and `align` of `slotProps.positioner` , and `sideOffset` specifies the distance from the trigger.

<Component path="hover-card/placement" />

When `arrow` is enabled, `sideOffset` defaults to 6 px to match the height of the arrow. `side` can be selected from `"top"` , `"bottom"` , `"left"` , `"right"` . If it is close to the edge of the screen, the position will be automatically adjusted to fit within the viewing area.

## Opening/closing control [#controlled]

By specifying `open` and `onOpenChange` , the open/closed state can be controlled externally. `delay` and `closeDelay` are specified in milliseconds. If you change `delay` to `0` , it will open immediately on hover.

<Component path="hover-card/controlled" />

Use `defaultOpen` to specify an uncontrolled initial state. If you want to process when the opening/closing animation is complete, you can use `onOpenChangeComplete` .

## Styling [#styling-hooks]

Popups are given `sui_hover_card_popup` . Class names such as `sui_hover_card_popup-size_md` , `sui_hover_card_popup-radius_md` , `sui_hover_card_popup-outlined` , `sui_hover_card_popup-open` , `sui_hover_card_popup-side_top` are also added depending on the props.

Triggers are given `sui_hover_card_trigger` , arrows are given `sui_hover_card_arrow` , and placed elements are given `sui_hover_card_positioner` . Popup margins and background color can be adjusted using `--sui_hover_card_popup-*` CSS variables.
