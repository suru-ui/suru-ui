Image is a component that displays an image. By default, `object-fit` is not specified and the image is displayed in its original proportions. You can use `fit` to specify how it fits into the container, and `radius` to specify rounded corners. The size is specified using `width` and `height` or `style` or CSS.

## Basic usage [#basic-usage]

Specify the image URL in `src` and the image content in `alt` . Specifying the size with `width` and `height` of `style` will make it easier to match the layout.

<Component path="image/basic" />

Images that convey content should be given descriptive `alt` . Combine `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` for decorative images to prevent screen readers from reading them.

## Display method and rounded corners [#fit-and-radius]

`fit` corresponds to `object-fit` , and you can choose from `cover` , `contain` , `fill` , `none` , `scale-down` . `radius` can be chosen from `none` , `sm` , `md` , `lg` , `full` .

<Component path="image/fit" />

`fit="cover"` fills the container without leaving any blank spaces and cuts off any excess. Since `fit="contain"` displays the entire image, there may be some blank space depending on the ratio of the image. If you want `radius="full"` to be circular, make `width` and `height` the same value.

## Alternative text and loading [#accessibility]

If you want to describe the content of the image, write a short and specific description in `alt` . Specifying `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"` allows you to delay the loading of off-screen images, making them less likely to interfere with drawing.

```tsx
<Image
  src="/images/landscape.jpg"
  alt="Mountains dyed in the sunset"
  fit="cover"
  radius="md"
  loading="lazy"
  decoding="async"
  style={{ width: "100%", height: 240 }}
/>
```

## Styling [#styling-hooks]

The root element is given `sui_image` . Class names such as `sui_image-radius_md` will be added depending on `radius` , `sui_image-fit_cover` will be added depending on `fit` , etc.

You can adjust the display using the `--sui_image-object_fit` , `--sui_image-border_radius` , and `--sui_image-corner_shape` CSS variables.
