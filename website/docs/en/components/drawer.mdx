A Drawer is an area that slides out from the edge of the screen. Use this when you want to display supplementary operations, settings, confirmations, etc. while keeping the current screen. You can specify the display edge and swipe direction with `swipeDirection` .

## Basic usage [#basic-usage]

Pass the element that opens the drawer to `trigger` and the contents to `title` , `description` , and `children` . `footer` is displayed fixedly at the bottom.

<Component path="drawer/basic" />

`showCloseIconButton` defaults to `true` with a close icon in the top right. `controls` allows you to add your own operations in front of it.

## Display direction [#direction]

`swipeDirection` can be `up` , `down` , `left` , `right` . When `showSwipeHandle` becomes `true` , swipe handles appear on the edge of the drawer.

<Component path="drawer/directions" />

`swipeDirection` also determines the direction of the swipe to close the drawer. `modal={false}` results in a non-modal drawer that allows you to interact with the screen behind it.

## snap point [#snap-points]

Specifying `snapPoints` allows you to stop the drawer at a fixed height. Numbers between 0 and 1 are treated as a percentage of the viewport height; numbers greater than 1 are treated as pixel values. Strings containing `px` and `rem` can also be specified.

```tsx
const snapPoints = ["148px", 0.5, 1];

<Drawer
  title="quick settings"
  trigger={<Button>Open settings<</Button>}
  snapPoints={snapPoints}
  defaultSnapPoint={0.5}
  onSnapPointChange={(snapPoint) => {
    console.log(snapPoint);
  }}
>
  You can change the height by dragging.
</Drawer>;
```

When `snapToSequentialPoints` is set to `true` , swiping moves to adjacent snap points in order.

## Styling [#styling-hooks]

Popups are given `sui_drawer_popup` . Depending on the state, class names such as `sui_drawer_popup-swipe_direction_down` , `sui_drawer_popup-open` , `sui_drawer_popup-swiping` , `sui_drawer_popup-expanded` , `sui_drawer_popup-nested` are also added.

Internal parts have class names such as `sui_drawer_content` , `sui_drawer_header` , `sui_drawer_title` , `sui_drawer_description` , `sui_drawer_body` , `sui_drawer_footer` , `sui_drawer_swipe_handle` , etc.
