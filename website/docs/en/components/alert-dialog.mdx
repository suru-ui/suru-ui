AlertDialog is a modal dialog that asks for confirmation before irreversible operations such as deletion. Darkens the background and blocks other actions until you choose to confirm or cancel. You can configure the title, description, icon, confirmation button, and cancel button all at once.

## Basic usage [#basic-usage]

Specify the element that opens the dialog in `trigger` and the confirmation content in `title` and `description` . `cancel` and `confirm` are the abort and confirm buttons respectively. Pressing either will close the dialog.

<Component path="alert-dialog/basic" />

If you pass an element to `children` , it will be displayed as the body below the description. Used to store supplementary information necessary for confirmation, such as a list of items to be deleted.

```tsx
<AlertDialog
  trigger={<Button color="red">delete<</Button>}
  title="Do you want to delete it?"
  description="This operation cannot be undone."
  cancel={<Button variant="naked">cancel<</Button>}
  confirm={<Button color="red">delete<</Button>}
>
  <p>List of items to be deleted.<</p>
</AlertDialog>
```

`Button` etc. can be passed as is to `confirm` and `cancel` . If you pass your own content to `footer` , `cancel` and `confirm` will still be placed after the footer content.

## Appearance [#appearance]

`variant` can be selected from `default` and `stacked` . `stacked` puts the heading and description in the center and stacks the operations in the top right. `size` , `radius` , `outlined` control the size, rounded corners, and borders of the dialog.

<Component path="alert-dialog/appearance" />

Passing an icon to `icon` will display it to the left of the header. The color scheme and background of the icon can be adjusted with `slotProps.icon` . If you omit `action` and change `showCancelIconButton` to `true` , a close icon button will appear in the top right corner.

## Controlled [#controlled]

`open` and `onOpenChange` allow you to control opening and closing of dialogs from external state. It is also possible to omit `trigger` and open it from any button.

<Component path="alert-dialog/controlled" />

By specifying `defaultOpen` , you can display it in the open state from the beginning. If you want to manipulate it imperatively, create a handle in `AlertDialog.createHandle()` and pass it to `handle` .

If you want to perform validation when the confirmation button is pressed, if there is a problem with `onConfirm` , you can call `event.preventBaseUIHandler()` to stop the process without closing the dialog.

## Styling [#styling-hooks]

Dialog popups are given `sui_alert_dialog_popup` and `sui_card` from cards. Depending on the props, class names such as `sui_alert_dialog_popup-size_md` , `sui_alert_dialog_popup-radius_md` , `sui_alert_dialog_popup-variant_stacked` , `sui_alert_dialog_popup-outlined` are also added.

Internal parts are given `sui_alert_dialog_backdrop` , `sui_alert_dialog_viewport` , `sui_alert_dialog_header` , `sui_alert_dialog_icon` , `sui_alert_dialog_title` , `sui_alert_dialog_description` , `sui_alert_dialog_content` , `sui_alert_dialog_footer` .

In addition to adjusting `className` and `style` , you can override margins and width using CSS variables.
